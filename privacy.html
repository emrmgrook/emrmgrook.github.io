Archangel - Privacy policy

Archangel is a personal app used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

  • Email is accessed only for the owner's own accounts, after the owner signs in and allows it.
  • Emails and sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, encrypted with the owner's Windows account.
  • No email data is sold, shared, or sent to any other person, company or server.
  • No advertising and no tracking.
  • The owner can remove access at any time in their Google, Microsoft or Yahoo account settings, or by removing the account in Archangel.

Google user data is used in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
